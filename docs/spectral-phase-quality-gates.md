# Spectral and phase compatibility quality gates

These are clean, deterministic compatibility gates. They do not certify
production perceptual quality and do not compare against proprietary encoder
output. The executable corpus is generated by
`cargo run -p jocforge -- spectral-phase OUTPUT.json`.

## Measurement contract

The repository-owned corpus has ten fixtures: broadband mono, identical
in-phase stereo, polarity-inverted anti-phase stereo, quadrature stereo,
highly correlated stereo, decorrelated stereo noise, an impulse, a
high-frequency transient, low-frequency coherent content, and a coherent
multi-object scene admitted through the N-to-15 reducer. Inputs are generated
from deterministic sine/cosine/noise functions and contain no copyrighted
audio.

The hard-gated reference stage is authored deterministic PCM. The clean
compatibility presentation applies a documented common normalized calibration
of `0.99` to both channels. This isolates spectral/phase invariants from
amplitude calibration. The multi-object reduction row is supplemental and is
required to be finite and bounded but is not assigned a hard spectral-quality
threshold. E-AC-3 decoded and OpenJOC-rendered stages are reported as
`NOT_AVAILABLE` by this pure corpus command; T-008 combines this evidence
with the actual controlled-profile E-AC-3/OpenJOC/FFmpeg checks.

FFT measurements use one 256-sample periodic Hann window at 48 kHz, with bins
0 through 128. Low/mid/high bands are bins 0..4, 5..31, and 32..128.
Inter-channel phase uses the highest-energy pair bin; correlation is cosine
similarity of the time-domain channels.

## Gates

| Gate | Metric | Clean controlled reference | Tolerance | Rationale | Failure mode detected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Magnitude spectrum | normalized RMS magnitude-spectrum deviation | authored pair versus common-gain presentation | `<= 0.02` | a 1% common calibration leaves explicit margin | broadband coloration or spectral loss hidden by RMS |
| Band energy | maximum relative low/mid/high band error | same pair, same window/bands | `<= 0.03` | power change from the 0.99 calibration is 1.99% | band-specific loss or boost |
| Spectral tilt | absolute log high/low ratio change | same pair | `<= 0.05` | common gain cancels from the ratio | low/high coloration |
| Low/high retention | per-band energy ratio | same pair | `0.95..=1.05` | explicit bounded energy retention | low- or high-frequency disappearance |
| Transient spectrum | magnitude-vector cosine retention | impulse/high-frequency transient | `>= 0.95` | transient shape must remain recognizable under calibration | transient smearing or spectral collapse |
| Phase/coherence | phase difference, correlation, stereo-width error, boundary discontinuity | in-phase, anti-phase, quadrature, and transient controls | finite; boundary/width `<= 0.02` | common gain preserves inter-channel phase and width | phase discontinuity, collapse, or incoherent stereo |
| Polarity | correlation sign | in-phase and anti-phase controls | positive remains `>= 0.99`; anti-phase remains `<= -0.99` | polarity is a causal invariant | accidental polarity inversion/collapse |
| Quadrature | absolute phase distance from `pi/2` and correlation magnitude | known 90-degree pair | phase error `<= 0.05` rad; `abs(correlation) <= 0.1` | quadrature must remain distinguishable from 0/180 degrees | quadrature collapse |
| Multi-object reduction | finite metrics; coded object count | 16 coherent authored sources through reducer | coded count `<= 15`; no non-finite value | structural evidence only; not a perceptual gate | reducer produces invalid or unbounded compatibility data |

## Supplemental Hanabi diagnostics

The closure run records the same metric families for representative Hanabi
windows when external rendered artifacts are available. These observations
are supplemental: they can identify suspicious coloration, coherence changes,
or phase discontinuities, but they do not change the controlled thresholds and
do not authorize perceptual tuning.
