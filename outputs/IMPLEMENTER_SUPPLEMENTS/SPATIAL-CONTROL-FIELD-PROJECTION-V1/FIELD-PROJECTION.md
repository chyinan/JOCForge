Grade: LOCAL_MODEL crosswalk from the approved F03W/GTC specifications and input-only vector identities. The source schema is `selected-adm-installed48-state.v3`; the supplied package does not define a standalone `spatial_control v5` byte schema. The mappings below are proposed projections of named F03W owned state, not claims about an existing v5 serializer or native pointers. Actual complete after-state must come from the corresponding staged producer and be committed only by its parent.

`Q=ceil(N/1536)` is the input schedule, not a substitute for every clock field. A normal short read pads the physical frame with positive zero; four drains reuse the final padded frame and control snapshot. F03W stages all owned changes and returns a proposal; the parent publishes a complete root once after all required stages/receipts succeed. A leaf proposal or receipt by itself is not a full root. Failure discards staged changes and leaves all prior owner bytes and clocks unchanged; retirement is a sideband. Full reset destroys owners and rebuilds their declared cold constructors.

External F05R56 metadata history/reader/targets and F04C component/LFE/receiver/QMF are distinct owners. No byte payload is reconstructed from hashes, equal values, callback adjacency or another owner's fields. Input-only vectors demonstrate synthetic selected-scope sequencing, not real ADM execution, production serialization or audio acceptance. Native pointer/owner inference is outside this crosswalk.
