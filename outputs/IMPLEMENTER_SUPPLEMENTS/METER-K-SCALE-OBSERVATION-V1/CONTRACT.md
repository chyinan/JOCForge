# Meter K scale: bounded observation

Selected reducer static analysis gives d=2*k: when d>31, subtract 31 and round q*2^-31 to F32; if remaining d>32 subtract 31 and repeat; then round q*2^-d to F32 before energy update. The defined scope is nonnegative k with normal finite factors, not arbitrary signed values. For k=0 and finite p with compatible floating-point environment, the operation is R32(p*1), matching direct p; k=1 gives R32(p/4). No general numeric or native floating environment equivalence is asserted.

Three authorized ADM jobs each yielded eight completed selected reducer calls, six channels per call, with k=0 at entry and return. Each call was tied to its observed local invocation/state; eight calls within each job used the same observed state and thread. Two input opens were observed; the first was not observed by the open hook. The second run had six observer bookkeeping errors after the eight-call limit, excluded from the reported observations. No power/PCM, full programme, nonzero-k sample or reset was observed. This supports the zero-scale identity only for those calls, not complete model equality or full-track behavior.
